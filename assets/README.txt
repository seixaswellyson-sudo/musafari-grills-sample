Hero video drop-in folder
=========================

Hero video files (hero-wide.mp4 desktop, hero-vertical.mp4 mobile) are
AI-generated illustrative footage (Google Flow): generic skewer grilling,
ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY. The hero always shows a visible "Illustrative video"
badge and never implies the footage is Musafari grills' own food.

All photographs are free stock images (Pexels), labelled as illustrative.
